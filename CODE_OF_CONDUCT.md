# Code of conduct

This project welcomes everyone who wants to use or improve it. To keep it that way:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagree politely and explain why.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory or sexual language or images.
- Do not share other people's private information.

Comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions that break these rules may be edited or removed, and repeated
or serious cases may lead to a ban from the project.

To report a problem, open a private report from the [Security tab](https://github.com/Sandiozo/runner-manager/security)
and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.
